COMPANY PROFILE

About us

Shenzhen Haoran Electronics Co., Ltd. was established in 2002 and operates together with Huizhou Shixin Technology Co., Ltd. The group has expanded its business and manufacturing network to Huai'an, Jiangsu and Nanning, Guangxi. Huizhou Shixin was established in 2022 in the China-Korea Emerging Manufacturing Industrial Park and now serves as the principal manufacturing entity, with approximately 3,000 square metres of production and office space and automated manufacturing and inspection equipment.

We focus on the engineering and manufacture of SMD power inductors and magnetic components. The product system covers molded inductors, shielded and unshielded power inductors, precision wound inductors, common-mode products, toroidal products and custom solutions for communication, consumer, automotive, security, medical, industrial and power-system applications.

Production and quality control follow ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 management systems together with RoHS and REACH material-compliance requirements. We support product selection, parameter review, sample validation and production introduction for standard and OEM/ODM projects.
