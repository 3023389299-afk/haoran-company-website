APPLICATION OVERVIEW

Application context
and selection logic

Adapters, industrial power supplies, server VRMs, graphics-card power rails and DC-DC modules require careful coordination of saturation current, DCR, core loss and thermal conditions.

Molded, shielded, flat-wire and through-hole inductors can be selected around topology, switching frequency and load dynamics.
